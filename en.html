The problem

The barrier isn't prompting skill — it's articulation

Every AI tool asks users open-ended questions they can't answer. Three sins of traditional prompt tools:

1

Interrogation

"What are your QA standards?" — users don't know. The articulation burden lands on the people least equipped to carry it.

2

One-size-fits-all

Every task gets "a better prompt". Complex work is underserved; simple work is over-engineered.

3

Deliver and vanish

No acceptance criteria, no verification, no iteration loop. Quality is luck; nothing compounds.

Quality by design

It sizes the job before doing the job

Small ask

One great prompt — copy, paste, done. No questions asked.

Multi-step

An SOP with a checkpoint at every step.

Complex

A staged pipeline — clean context per stage, checks between stages.

Heavy

An AI team with divided roles that review each other.

Four safeguards on every delivery: acceptance criteria agreed up front · critical claims cross-checked · an adversarial reviewer before shipping · a hard stop-loss after three rounds. Quality is designed, not lucked into.
